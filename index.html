Our Story

Hi, I'm the maker behind Angawey. 💛

I fell in love with copper — not just for its warm, glowing beauty, but for what it does for the body. Copper has been worn for centuries for its health benefits, and it's one of the most versatile metals to craft with. But too often, people who want copper jewellery can't find where to buy it, and small copper makers can't reach the people who'd love their work.

So Angawey was born — a friendly little home for handmade copper jewellery, and a bridge between you and the makers who pour their hearts into every piece. Every item is made by hand, with care, and shipped with love.

Thank you for being here. It truly means the world. 🧡
